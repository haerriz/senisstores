# Senisstores SEO and conversion audit — 2026-11-19

## Analytics baseline (22 Oct–18 Nov 2026)

- 69 sessions, 35 engaged sessions, 50.72% engagement rate and ₹0 tracked revenue.
- Organic Search: 11 sessions, 81.82% engagement, 2m 02s average engagement.
- Organic Shopping: 21 sessions, 90.48% engagement, 56s average engagement.
- Direct: 31 sessions, 9.68% engagement, 1s average engagement; much of this is low-quality or unattributed traffic.
- GA4 contained only automatic events (`page_view`, `user_engagement`, `session_start`, `first_visit`, `scroll`). It had no ecommerce funnel events.
- Store database: no orders in 90 days; 19 active carts with products in the last 28 days, only one with an email address.

## Root causes found

1. Traffic volume is too low to support consistent sales, despite strong engagement from organic visitors.
2. A global homepage canonical was injected on every page, making products/categories appear to be homepage duplicates.
3. Title prefix and suffix repeated the brand around every page title.
4. All 46 products lacked meta descriptions; ten lacked meta titles and existing titles were duplicated.
5. Homepage content had a wrong phone number, thin local content and no reliable, page-scoped local schema.
6. GA4 could not report product views, cart additions, checkout starts or purchases.
7. `robots.txt` did not declare the XML sitemap.

## Changes deployed

- Added correct canonical URLs for homepage, products and categories.
- Removed duplicate/manual GA and invalid global metadata injection.
- Added one verified `HardwareStore` schema on the homepage using the configured Theni address, phone, hours and map coordinates.
- Rewrote homepage title, description and local content for Theni and nearby towns, while retaining India-wide delivery intent.
- Corrected the visible phone number and added retail/bulk-order/service information.
- Added unique titles and meta descriptions to all 46 products.
- Added GA4 `view_item`, `add_to_cart`, `view_cart`, `begin_checkout` and `purchase` events with prescribed item and transaction parameters.
- Added a crawl-safe `robots.txt` with the sitemap declaration.

## Production verification

- Homepage, representative product, cart and checkout return HTTP 200.
- Homepage has one H1, one canonical, one GA measurement ID and one local-business schema block.
- Representative product has a self-referencing canonical, unique title/description and `view_item` configuration.
- Cart exposes `view_cart`; checkout tracking loads when a cart can enter checkout.
- Production revision: `ed0618752`.

## Follow-up measurement

GA4 reports are not retroactive. Review the ecommerce funnel after 14–28 days of real traffic. Search-result title/description updates depend on Google recrawling; they are not immediate or guaranteed. For local visibility, keep the Google Business Profile address, phone, hours and website URL identical to the site.
